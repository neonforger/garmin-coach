description: Review of the last 7 days (training + recovery) vs. your recent history
---

Use the `coach` agent to review the last 7 days (or the period in $ARGUMENTS):

- sessions table: date, type, distance, time, pace, avg HR, time in zones, training effect
- key sessions in detail (rebuild interval reps from laps)
- weekly totals vs. the average of the previous 4 weeks
- recovery: resting HR, sleep, body battery / readiness trends
- 3–5 bullet takeaways and, at the end, any questions the data can't answer

If the data in `data/` doesn't cover the period, fetch the missing days from the Garmin MCP.
